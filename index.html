THE DOUBLER

Two is double trouble

To multiply by 2, just double the number. It’s the same as adding it to itself!

2 × 6 = 6 + 6 = 12
×4
DOUBLE TWICE

Double, then double again

Find the 4 table by doubling once for 2, then doubling your answer again.

4 × 3 → 6 → 12
×5
FIVE FINGERS

Ends in 0 or 5

Every answer in the 5 table ends in a 5 or a 0. Count by fives to find out!

5, 10, 15, 20, 25!
×9
NINE'S NEAT TRICK

Digits add up to 9

In the 9 table, the digits in every answer add up to 9. Try 9 × 4 = 36: 3 + 6 = 9!

9 × 4 = 36 → 3 + 6 = 9
×10
ADD A ZERO

Zero makes it easy

To multiply by 10, just pop a zero at the end of the number. Easy peasy!

10 × 8 = 8 + 0 = 80
×11
DOUBLE UP

The repeating eleven

For 11 × a number under 10, say that number twice. What a cool pattern!

11 × 7 = 77 · 11 × 3 = 33
×12
A TEN AND A TWO

Ten times, plus two times

Split 12 into 10 + 2. Multiply each part, then add the answers together.

12 × 4 = 40 + 8 = 48
